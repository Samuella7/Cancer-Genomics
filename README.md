# Cancer-Genomics Revoir 2 et 4
 Project Aim: This study seeks to understand how specific genetic variations, particularly SNPs, contribute to  the development and progression of brain cancer in individuals

Materials and Methods
Functional Annotation
Genome annotation was conducted on the human genome by aligning genes from a reference genome, specifically chromosome 10 from the RefSeq database (in GenBank format), to the subject dataset. The subject dataset consisted of raw data from an SRR14994031 paired-end sequencing run of a brain cancer patient, from which final contig assemblies were derived (Sample 1) .
The reference mapping facilitated the identification and transfer of genes, enabling the annotation of genomic variants within the annotated human genome features. Annotations included Coding Sequences (CDS), Genes, RNA, and other functional elements, leveraging insights from previous reference mapping analyses.
Additionally, gene feature predictions and analyses, such as those for promoter regions and regulatory sequences, were performed to provide a comprehensive understanding of the functional landscape of the human genome.
The results, summarized in Table 1, highlight the abundance and diversity of genomic elements identified within the dataset.
